# VLA, video and semantic understanding: paper collection

Selected by relevance to generalizable real-world action, video, semantic/spatial understanding, or practical training. There is no verified universal “best” model: results depend on robot, data, task, hardware and protocol. Entries summarize source abstracts and project materials unless stated otherwise; numerical claims should be checked in the original experiments before use. Collection date: 28 October 2026.

## Foundations and practical baselines

| Paper | Why it matters | Evidence and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [OpenVLA](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/kim25c.html), Kim et al., 2025 proceedings | Open 7B VLA trained on diverse robot demonstrations; a useful reference for action prediction and adaptation. | Published; authors report real-robot results and fine-tuning; [code](https://github.com/openvla/openvla). Earlier foundational work, not necessarily the latest or strongest. |
| [OpenVLA-OFT](https://openvla-oft.github.io/), 2025 | Practical fine-tuning recipe using action chunks and continuous action prediction. | Authors report simulation and real-robot experiments; a recipe for adapting OpenVLA, not a separate foundation model. |
| [π0.5: a Vision-Language-Action Model with Open-World Generalization](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v305/black25a.html), Black et al., 2025 | Co-trains robot actions with semantic subtask prediction, detection and web data; explicitly targets new environments. | Published; authors demonstrate selected tasks in new homes. [OpenPI](https://github.com/Physical-Intelligence/openpi) offers checkpoints and examples; it is not an exact reproduction of all original data and deployment. |
| [SmolVLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01844), 2025 | Smaller VLA intended for accessible robot learning and adaptation. | Preprint source; [LeRobot training and deployment guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/main/smolvla). Check robot and checkpoint compatibility. |
| [Qwen-VLA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.30280), Wang et al., 2026 | Joint training on robot trajectories, human demonstrations, navigation and other data, with robot-specific conditioning. | 2026 preprint; authors report simulation, navigation and real ALOHA tests. [Project repository](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-VLA); verify available weights and training support before choosing it. |

## Video, physical prediction and semantics

| Paper | Why it matters | Evidence and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [UniVLA: Learning to Act Anywhere with Task-centric Latent Actions](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.06111), Bu et al., RSS 2025 | Learns task-centred latent actions from video across embodiments, then decodes them into robot actions. | Real robot and benchmark results reported; linked code. **Distinct from the other UniVLA below.** |
| [Unified Vision-Language-Action Model](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.19850), Wang et al., ICLR 2026 | Jointly models vision, language and action tokens, incorporating video and world modeling. | Authors report simulation and real ALOHA experiments; [code](https://github.com/baaivision/UniVLA). Also called UniVLA, but a different paper and team. |
| [Spatial-Aware VLA Pretraining through Visual-Physical Alignment from Human Videos](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Feng_Spatial-Aware_VLA_Pretraining_through_Visual-Physical_Alignment_from_Human_Videos_CVPR_2026_paper.html), Feng et al., CVPR 2026 | VIPA-VLA connects human-video-derived 3D visual and action supervision with spatial understanding. Particularly relevant to the geometry/AROS connection. | Published; assess reconstruction assumptions and target embodiment in the paper. |
| [Being-H0: Vision-Language-Action Pretraining from Large-Scale Human Videos](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v306/luo26e.html), Luo et al., ICML 2026 | Uses large-scale human video, physical alignment and robot adaptation to learn manipulation-relevant representations. | Published; reported transfer remains bounded by the evaluated tasks and robots. |
| [CoT-VLA: Visual Chain-of-Thought Reasoning for Vision-Language-Action Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Zhao_CoT-VLA_Visual_Chain-of-Thought_Reasoning_for_Vision-Language-Action_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.html), Zhao et al., CVPR 2025 | Predicts future visual goals before action chunks, connecting temporal prediction and control. | Published; authors report simulated and real manipulation results. Predicted frames are not proof of physical feasibility. |
| [Actions as Language: Fine-Tuning VLMs into VLAs Without Catastrophic Forgetting](https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2026/hash/7a0f8055c838df8e62329a76c7c6403d-Abstract-Conference.html), Hancock et al., ICLR 2026 | VLM2VLA addresses loss of semantic capabilities during robot adaptation by representing actions as language and using LoRA. | Published; authors report visual question answering and real-robot experiments. Crucial for testing whether task learning degrades scene understanding. |
| [ORION: Vision-based Manipulation from Single Human Video with Open-World Object Graphs](https://ut-austin-rpl.github.io/ORION-release/), 2026 journal version | Builds object relations, keypoints and action-relevant geometry from human video; links closely to transferable affordances. | Author project; real tabletop experiments use particular perception and grasping assumptions. |
| [ChronoGraph: Functional 4D Scene Graphs with Vision-Language Models for Interaction Understanding and Grounded Planning](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.39665), Zhang et al., 2026 | Links affordance parts and actions to semantic and geometric state changes, a particularly close fit to the proposed research direction. | Recent preprint; real-world demonstrations use existing robot skills, not a new end-to-end controller. |
| [Cosmos Policy](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/dir/cosmos-policy/), ICLR 2026 | Adapts a video diffusion model with robot demonstrations for actions, future states and value estimates. | Research project; world-model computation and deployment requirements matter. |
| [Qwen-RobotWorld](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17030), Zhang et al., 2026 | Language-conditioned future video model spanning embodied settings; useful for understanding predictive world models and data generation. | 2026 technical report/preprint. It is a video world model, not by itself a verified robot controller. |
| [VPP2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.10270), 2026 | Recent video world–action model; worth tracking for the link between visual prediction and policy. | Recent preprint; author-reported results require careful independent assessment. |

## Data and overview

| Source | Why read it | Evidence and access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Open X-Embodiment](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.08864), 2023 | Diverse robot datasets to study cross-robot training; action spaces and cameras must be reconciled. | Foundational dataset paper. |
| [DROID](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.12945), 2024 | Large real-world manipulation dataset with variation in scenes and tasks. | Paper and [policy training code](https://github.com/droid-dataset/droid_policy_learning). |
| [Spa-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12696), 2026 | Tests whether VLAs can ground spatial language in real tabletop actions under changed layouts. | Recent preprint; reports drops on held-out arrangements for three evaluated policies, within its particular SO-101 tasks. |
| [From Human Videos to Robot Manipulation](https://ijcai.org/proceedings/2026/871), Feng et al., IJCAI 2026 | Survey organizing latent actions, world models, 2D cues and 3D reconstruction. | Published survey, useful for expanding the reading list; not a primary experiment. |

## Search and screening notes

Sources: web and arXiv search, paper and conference pages, authors' project sites and official repositories; searched 28 October 2026. Representative queries included `2026 vision language action robotics model video understanding semantic generalization`, `robot vision language action video pretraining semantic understanding physical reasoning`, and `UniVLA latent action video robot`, followed by source and citation checks. Selected papers combine relevance, representative methods, recency and accessibility. This is a curated scoping review; no comprehensive result count or systematic screening was performed, and most entries have been reviewed at abstract or project-page level. Preprint labels refer to the linked source and do not assert that a later peer-reviewed version does not exist.
